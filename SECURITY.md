# Security Policy

J & M International Group LLC (d/b/a Tenebrae Security) takes the security of its products seriously. This policy applies to every repository in the `J-M-International-Group` organization and to the TENEBRAE product line: Guard, Solo, Mobile, Command and Privacy Guardian.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for a security problem.

Email **contact@tenebraesecurity.com** with the subject line `Security report`. Include:

- The product, version and platform affected
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact as you understand it
- How you would like to be credited, if at all

You will receive an acknowledgement within **3 business days**. We aim to confirm or rule out the issue within 10 business days and to keep you informed while a fix is developed.

## Scope

In scope:

- TENEBRAE Guard, Solo, Mobile, Command and Privacy Guardian software
- The APIs and consoles that ship with those products
- `tenebraesecurity.com` and its subdomains

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service or volumetric testing against hosted services
- Social engineering of staff, customers or partners
- Findings that require physical access to a customer's device
- Third-party services we do not operate (app stores, DNS or hosting providers)

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith: test only against accounts and data they own or are authorized to use, avoid privacy violations and service degradation, and give us reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are released for the current production version of each product. Customers on older versions are asked to upgrade.
