**TENEBRAE Security** is the cybersecurity product line of **J & M International Group LLC**, a United States company. We defend the two surfaces existing security tools can't see: the AI your business runs on, and the mobile devices your people work from. Detection runs on your own infrastructure.

## Products

### AI security

| Product | What it does | Built for |
|---|---|---|
| [**TENEBRAE Guard**](https://www.tenebraesecurity.com/guard) | Self-hosted AI runtime security. Inspects every prompt before it reaches your model, and it runs on your infrastructure, so raw prompts never leave your building. | Enterprise and MSP AI teams |
| [**TENEBRAE Solo**](https://www.tenebraesecurity.com/solo) | Hosted AI workflow protection. Add one HTTP step to Make.com, Zapier, n8n or your own code and every prompt gets screened before it runs. | Developers and automation builders |
| [**Privacy Guardian**](https://www.tenebraesecurity.com/guardian) | A seatbelt for talking to AI. Catches personal data before you send it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, and flags scams in what comes back. *(Early access)* | Everyday people using AI assistants |

### Device security

| Product | What it does | Built for |
|---|---|---|
| [**TENEBRAE Mobile**](https://www.tenebraesecurity.com/mobile) | Mobile threat defense for iOS, Android and ChromeOS, paired with the TENEBRAE Command console. On-device detection, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, automated response. | SOC teams, MSSPs and CISOs |

## How we build

- **On-device and self-hosted first.** Detection models run where the data already is, not in someone else's cloud.
- **Prompt injection, data leakage and mobile threats are one problem.** Attackers move across AI, browsers and endpoints; so do we.
- **Standards we hold ourselves to.** Signed and notarized desktop builds, continuous integration, dependency scanning, and a published vulnerability disclosure process (see our [security policy](https://github.com/J-M-International-Group/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).

Product source code lives in private repositories in this organization. Open-source components will appear here as they are released.
